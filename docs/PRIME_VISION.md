# PRIME Vision

PRIME is intended to become an AI operating company and platform whose defining capability is **continuous governed evolution**.

The platform is not limited to one model provider. It coordinates models, tools, memory, retrieval, workflows, simulations, evaluations, and product agents. Its differentiator is the ability to measure weaknesses, generate bounded alternatives, test them against protected benchmarks, and promote only improvements.

## Long-term capability levels

1. Prompt and response-policy evolution
2. Workflow and agent-topology evolution
3. Tool-selection and model-routing evolution
4. Retrieval and memory-policy evolution
5. Specialized fine-tuning and synthetic-data generation
6. Product capability proposal and implementation branches
7. Cross-product learning under tenant isolation
8. Customer-controlled deployment across cloud, private cloud, and on-premises environments

## PRIME products

- Guardian X
- Genome
- Company Brain
- Digital Twin

Each product supplies domain observations and benchmarks to the PRIME control plane. PRIME returns tested, versioned improvements without bypassing governance.
