# PRIME Vision

PRIME is intended to become a model-agnostic AI operating platform whose defining capability is controlled, measurable, and reversible evolution.

The platform coordinates models, tools, memory, retrieval, workflows, simulations, evaluations, and product agents. It should eventually support ChatGPT- and Claude-class user experiences and Cursor-class development workflows, but it must earn those capabilities incrementally through benchmarks, product usage, safety controls, and reliable infrastructure.

## Capability ladder

1. Persistent prompt and response-policy evolution
2. Workflow and agent-topology evolution
3. Tool selection, retrieval, memory, and model routing
4. Protected evaluations and adversarial testing
5. Canary releases, rollback, observability, and cost governance
6. Product-specific agents for Guardian X, Genome, Company Brain, and Digital Twin
7. Multi-tenant knowledge and customer-controlled deployment
8. Specialized fine-tuning and synthetic-data generation
9. Human-reviewed product capability proposals and implementation branches
10. Enterprise-scale reliability, security, privacy, and compliance

PRIME v0.2 establishes levels 1 through 5 at prototype scale. It is not yet a frontier foundation model or a finished commercial platform.
